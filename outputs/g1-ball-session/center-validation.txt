Local contour ellipse refinement — validation

Method: YOLO region -> padded original grayscale ROI -> local contrast check ->
three interpolated intensity contours -> ellipse fit -> shape and center agreement
checks -> stereo reprojection and table-volume checks. No temporal smoothing.
Subpixel interpolation does not create new sensor detail.

Replay comparison on identical samples, same raw camera-to-table poses:
Point 1, approximate tape Y 38.5 cm, 616 accepted / 0 rejected:
  Box Y median 40.249 cm; Y std 1.785 mm.
  Contour Y median 41.070 cm; Y std 1.074 mm.
Point 2, approximate tape Y 53 cm, 576 accepted / 0 rejected:
  Box Y median 53.964 cm; Y std 14.030 mm.
  Contour Y median 55.109 cm; Y std 0.834 mm.
Replay counts may include trailing buffered packets beyond the earlier CSV snapshot.

Conclusion: large stability improvement at point 2, but larger Y bias at both
measured points. This is not a demonstrated absolute-accuracy improvement.
Retain Box center as the default. Contour center (experimental) can be selected
in Viser; both world positions are displayed and logged. Missing or rejected
contours produce no refined measurement rather than falling back silently.

Synthetic tests passed for a subpixel circle, flat image, undersized ROI and
clipped ROI. Live packet processing and both Viser selector states verified.
Dynamic ball, blur, occlusion and broader distance range remain unvalidated.
